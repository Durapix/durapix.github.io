## 🚀 Featured project: [Chalkback](https://github.com/Durapix/chalkback)

> Tutoring whiteboards in, editable boards and lesson recaps out.

A learning platform for tutors. Past lesson boards (draw.io exports, screenshots, PDFs) become
live, editable [tldraw](https://tldraw.dev) whiteboards. Each lesson gets a recap for the student
that actually understands the board (flowcharts are read as algorithms), and the recap reaches
the student before the next lesson by e-mail, Telegram or a phone notification.

It is also an experiment in **agentic engineering**: the whole thing was built, reviewed, tested,
released, documented and deployed by Claude Code on autopilot, with me setting the goals,
constraints and priorities. Branches, pull requests, CI, releases and an Ansible-managed
production server, all in the open.
